# The Capita rules in short

Draft. Summary of the rules diary v0.24 (16 November 2026); where the two differ, the diary wins. The numbers live in [`rules/alpha.toml`](../rules/alpha.toml); what the rules do at 100, 1 000 and 100 000 members is in [rule-check.md](rule-check.md).

## What Capita is

Capita is an experiment on the effect of an income that every person receives, the same everywhere in the world. It is not (yet) meant as a second currency. One Capita is roughly worth one euro or one dollar; this guide value only helps to set prices. Everything stays as simple as possible: to build, to use and to explain.

## Who takes part

1. **One person, one account, for life.** A person is identified by name and date of birth from an identity document. The same person always lands on the same account, whichever identity service checks them.
2. **Verified means: document checked.** The phone reads the chip of the passport or ID card and compares a selfie with the chip photo. The check only counts from a real, unaltered phone with the Capita app. If our own check fails, a paid outside service (Didit) is the backup.
3. **Any age.** Passports exist from birth, so there is no minimum age.
4. **Every year again.** Each member verifies once a year, around the birthday. This handles death, leaving and expired documents.

## How much Capita a person receives

5. **Your age in Capita.** On the day of verification a person receives **1 × their age** at once. On every birthday after that they receive **2 × their age** at once. Examples: verified at 30 → 30 Capita; on turning 31 → 62 Capita. A baby receives almost nothing, a pensioner the most: the Capita of children pay for raising them, the Capita of old people act as a pension.
6. **One factor for the world.** The 2 is a single global value. It may be changed later, or set per country, if research on how much free money people can receive before they stop working says so.
7. **No monthly amount, no income test.** Capita come only by age.
8. **Only while verified.** A birthday pays only if the member's yearly verification is up to date. Capita once received never expire.
9. **Start.** A new account receives 1 Capita, which pays for its own verification. Sponsors can pay verifications for others (1 € = 1 verification) and are named publicly.

## Holding and spending

10. **No debt.** No account can go below zero. There are no loans or credit lines in the system. Friends simply send each other Capita.
11. **No decay, no fees.** Capita do not lose value over time, and the system takes nothing from a payment. Spam is held off by a free number of transactions per verified person instead of fees.
12. **Step 1: digital goods only.** At first only digital goods and services may be sold for Capita (e-books, music, software, courses …). Real goods follow in steps, where enough supply is assured. Whatever is illegal under the applicable law is banned.
13. **Euros in, never out.** Anyone may buy Capita with euros (1 € = 1 Capita, up to 100 € per payment); bought Capita can be spent once the buyer is verified. Capita can never be exchanged back into euros or any other currency.

## When something goes wrong

14. **Lost phone or key.** Verify again and the account moves to the new key after 7 days. During those days the old key can stop the move; then the account is locked until the board checks the person (for example by video call with the document).
15. **Double account.** If one person turns out to have two accounts, the younger one is closed and its balance deleted. The older one stays untouched.
16. **Selling copies of others' work, or breaking shop rules.** 1st time: a warning, and the buyer is compensated with the price. 2nd: one calendar month without selling. 3rd and later: one month without buying, selling, sending or receiving Capita and without chats – but the member keeps receiving birthday Capita, can still vote and can see everything. The count starts again after 2 years without a new case. Whoever knowingly sells a stolen work starts at the 2nd step; whoever proves it receives the price as a reward. Earnings from a new work wait 14 days before they can be spent.
17. **Inactive accounts.** Without verification an account is frozen; nothing is lost. After 10 years without verification it closes: Capita received by age are destroyed, Capita bought with euros are kept for the community.

## Openness and decisions

18. **Open totals, private people.** All totals and every payment between addresses are public, without names. Each member keeps their own data (photos, chats) on their own device. The screens never show keys, codes or technical data.
19. **Who changes the rules.** Now: the founder alone, in a public diary, announced ahead and never backdated. After the association (e.V.) is founded: its board. Later: a vote of all verified members, one person one vote ("glassroots").
20. **Worldwide from day one**, except countries excluded by law (for example sanctions). The app is in English and German. Before the public beta the ledger runs on at least 4 computers of at least 3 independent operators.

## Not decided yet

- **Paying in shops and restaurants** with the phone, the merchant receiving euros: wanted, but it conflicts with step 1 (digital goods only) and with "never out". Where would the merchant's euros come from?
- **Who pays verifications** when there are not enough sponsors.
- **Sellers who need euros** for real costs (postage, material) – only after the legal check.
- **Children's accounts** (who holds the key) and **the age visible on a public ledger** – legal check.
- **Face comparison against duplicates** with two documents of one person – built only after the legal check.
- **What "the community" is** that receives money of closed accounts, and what heirs receive.
